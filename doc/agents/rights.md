# Rights

**Status:** 🧪 beta

> Which societies to join, which numbers to get, and a clean list of your songs with their splits — so every dollar a play generates has a door to reach you through.

## In a real label

**Who.** At a major this is business affairs and legal, plus a music publisher for the song side. At an independent, it is the label manager with a publisher or a publishing administrator beside them ([label-fundamentals](https://github.com/chloepilonv/house-label/blob/main/agents/label-manager/knowledge/label-fundamentals.md)). The publisher's job, in Berklee's words: "properly registering compositions with a performing-rights organization", "licensing compositions for use by films, television, advertisements", and "collecting and distributing royalties to clients" ([Berklee, Music Publisher](https://online.berklee.edu/career-roles-in-music/music-publisher), read 2026-10-25).

**Week to week.** One split sheet per song before release; the song registered with the performing-rights society; the recording registered on the master side; identifiers (ISRC, ISWC, IPI, IPN, UPC) collected and copied into every form; statements checked; a cover on a physical pressing licensed through CMRRA ([release-path](https://github.com/chloepilonv/house-label/blob/main/agents/label-manager/knowledge/release-path.md)).

**Why it is three jobs, not one.** One stream pays three rights through three doors: the song (writer and composer), the performance on the recording, and the recording itself. Missing one is invisible from the other two ([rights-fundamentals](https://github.com/chloepilonv/house-label/blob/main/agents/rights/knowledge/rights-fundamentals.md)).

**The societies, Quebec and Canada first.** Each one has its own page; the fee is what that page said on the read date.

| Society | Collects | For | Cost | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **SOCAN** | performance of the song, Canada-wide; Quebec has no separate society | writers, publishers — one work published, recorded or performed publicly to join | free | [socan.com](https://www.socan.com/sign-up-for-performing-rights/), read 2026-09-08 |
| **SOCAN Reproduction Rights** | mechanical (streams, downloads, CDs, vinyl) | opt in separately from SOCAN performing | 7% online · 10% physical/sync | [socan.com](https://www.socan.com/what-are-socan-reproduction-rights/), 2026-08-14 |
| **CMRRA** | mechanical — the other reproduction-rights door; SOCAN completed its purchase of CMRRA on 2026-09-01 and CMRRA keeps operating | publishers and self-published writers | not published | [cmrra.ca](https://www.cmrra.ca/about/) · [SOCAN announcement](https://www.socan.com/socan-announces-completion-of-cmrra-acquisition-2/), 2026-09-08 |
| **Artisti** | performer's neighbouring rights (radio, background music, webcasters) and the performer number (IPN) | any performer on an eligible recording | no membership fee; management fee off royalties, rate not published | [artisti.ca](https://www.artisti.ca/en/membership-documents/), 2026-09-08 |
| **Panorama (SOPROQ)** | the master owner's neighbouring rights — the legal entity is SOPROQ, the brand is Panorama; also issues Canadian ISRC prefixes | whoever paid for the recording — you, if you did | free; 7% Canada · 4% US · 7% rest of world | [panorama.mu](https://panorama.mu/en/about) · [ISRC](https://panorama.mu/en/isrc), 2026-09-08 |
| **SoundExchange** | US digital radio and webcasting, performer half and master half | non-US artists join directly; file a W-8BEN or 30% is withheld | free | [soundexchange.com](https://www.soundexchange.com/digital-performance-royalties/), 2026-09-08 |
| **MétaMusique** | not a society — Quebec's shared tool to document works, recordings and credits, get or check an ISNI, and make the BAnQ legal deposit; founded by SOCAN, Artisti, Panorama, UDA, GMMQ, ADISQ and peers | any Quebec release | price not stated on the site; support sessions free | [metamusique.ca](https://metamusique.ca/), read 2026-10-25 |

Also on the checklist: ACTRA RACS or Re:Sound direct as the other performer doors (pick **one** — two performer societies block the money until unwound), The MLC for US mechanicals (free, Canadians join directly), and ASCAP *or* BMI for a US writer — never both ([rights-fundamentals](https://github.com/chloepilonv/house-label/blob/main/agents/rights/knowledge/rights-fundamentals.md)).

**What it costs to hire it.** A publishing administrator registers everywhere for you and takes a cut: Songtrust charges "a flat, one-time fee" of US$100 per songwriter plus "15% for performance royalties and 20% for non-performance" ([songtrust.com/pricing](https://www.songtrust.com/pricing), read 2026-10-25). A publisher takes a share of the song — by convention up to half, then the rest splits lyricist/composer; no society publishes a default ([rights-fundamentals](https://github.com/chloepilonv/house-label/blob/main/agents/rights/knowledge/rights-fundamentals.md)). A lawyer for the agreements: varies — ask for a quote.

**Where the line is.** Distribution usually issues your ISRC and UPC at upload. The label manager builds the credits document. Sync licensing has no society and no desk here. Anything that is *what should I sign* is a lawyer's — this desk explains the clause and stops.

## Here

The agent reads your profile (country, province, writer / performer / owns the masters) and prints the per-side checklist before asking anything: composition side, then master side, each society with its own page, cost, requirement and tax form. It keeps your member numbers and IPI once you report them, and it keeps the song catalog: titles, identifiers, writer shares and master splits, each side warned when it does not total 100%.

| Skill | What you get | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Which societies to join | the checklist grouped by side, the exclusive choices (ASCAP or BMI; which mandate holds your reproduction right) named as decisions, and the eligibility bar stated before the steps — SOCAN wants works already public; then your member IDs kept, "registered" refused until the required fields are filled | ✅ developed |
| Your song catalog | the song and release list with hygiene flags; a song added by hand or a whole catalog from a file — both split sides, share totals checked by a script, every merge shown row by row and never overwriting what you typed | ✅ developed |

**What it never does**
- Files a registration or sends anything to a society — you do that on the society's own site.
- Drafts a contract or says what to agree to. "A producer point is 1% of the master" is knowledge; "give them three" is negotiation.
- Picks ASCAP or BMI for you — it gives the difference, then the pick is yours.
- Invents an identifier. A missing ISRC is a flag, never a guess; a field a file cannot be read from stays marked MISSING.
- Sends an independent to CONNECT — its service is for the majors.

**What it needs from you.** On file: country, province, roles, member numbers and IPI, your songs. It asks: whether you paid for the recording (that makes you the master owner), who changed what on each song (a producer who rewrote a verse is a writer), and which society or publisher already holds a song's reproduction right.

**Example** — invented artist.

> **Camille, Sherbrooke, writes and sings, paid for her own EP:** "Am I collecting everything? I'm only with SOCAN."
> **Rights:** "You have three sides and one door open. Composition: SOCAN, done; add SOCAN Reproduction Rights or CMRRA for mechanicals — one or the other holds the mandate. Performer: Artisti, ACTRA RACS or Re:Sound direct — pick one, two blocks the money. Master: Panorama, since you paid for the session. Then SoundExchange and The MLC for the US, with a W-8BEN or 30% is withheld. Fees are what each page said in September; confirm on the page. Record your Artisti number when you have it?"

Open it: `houselabel rights`.
