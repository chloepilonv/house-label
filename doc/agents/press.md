# Press

**Status:** 🧪 beta

> Gets your record **written about** — the journalists, the pitch, the follow-ups.

## In a real label

**Who does it.** A publicist. Berklee's definition: publicists "generate media
attention and manage public relations for musical artists and music-related
businesses", in-house at a label, at a PR firm, or freelance
([Berklee](https://www.berklee.edu/careers/roles/publicist)). At an independent
label it is the `press@` inbox on the contact page
([Sub Pop](https://www.subpop.com/contact)).

**Week to week.** They write the press release, build the media list, pitch one
writer at a time, offer one outlet the premiere, book interviews, and follow up.
Journalists want this: 66% rely on PR-provided material for story ideas, and 82%
reject a pitch that is off their beat
([Cision, State of the Media 2026](https://www.prnewswire.com/content/dam/prnewswire/resources/white-papers/Cision_2026_State_of_the_Media_Report.pdf)).

**What they produce.** A master pitch under 200 words, a one-page press release,
and coverage: a review, a feature, an interview, a premiere. The coverage
outlives the campaign — it is the pull-quote in the next presskit and grant
application.

**What it costs.** A publicist works on a monthly retainer, usually a 4–6-month
contract around a release, and "there are no guarantees — PR is not
advertising" ([Ari's Take](https://aristake.com/whats-a-publicist-and-should-i-get-one/)).
No trade body in Canada or the US publishes a rate. Vendors' own rate cards
cluster at USD 1,500–5,000 a month with 2–4-month minimums — the vendors'
account, not a benchmark. Ask for a quote.

**The line with the next desk.** Press gets you *written about*. Radio and
playlists get you *played* — that is [Promotion](./promotion), a different
pipeline with different gatekeepers
([Berklee](https://www.berklee.edu/careers/roles/promotion-manager)). The
presskit the pitch links to is built by [Presskit](./presskit); press uses it.

**Who is on a press list.** Dailies and their culture desks, the public
broadcaster, culture TV, alt-weeklies, national music press, specialist sites and
blogs, podcasts — and **magazines as their own group** (ELLE Québec, Clin d'œil,
and their equivalents elsewhere). A list that files magazines under "press" or
"blogs" forgets them; here they are listed separately every time.

## Here

The Press Agent writes one master pitch per release in your own first-person
voice (French or English), adapts it for each contact, tells you where you are on
the release timeline — 8 weeks before to 8 weeks after — and tracks every pitch
to a yes, a no, or silence. Open it with `houselabel press`.

| Skill | What you get | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Draft the pitch | the master pitch, 120–250 words, six beats in order (hook → who → release → credibility → details → one ask); a missing fact becomes an UPPERCASE placeholder, never a question | developed |
| Compose outreach | that pitch adapted for one contact — a daily's news angle, a blog's "for fans of" line, a TV desk's clip — in the contact's language, opened as a Gmail draft | developed |
| Manage contacts | the media list for your markets (Quebec, Canada, US), filtered by type, tier, region and language; says plainly which of your edits it can keep | developed |
| Run the campaign | what to do this week from the release date, the pipeline per contact, and a two-follow-up cap | developed |

**What it never does**

- Sends. Every email opens as a draft in your Gmail; you press send.
- Pitches radio or playlists — it names the Promotion Agent and hands off.
- Writes to a personal address (Gmail, Hotmail) found by a tool, or to anyone
  who said "stop" — Quebec, Canadian and US law, quoted in its knowledge file.
- Spends your Hunter credits without asking first, with the number.
- Invents a stat, a quote, a credit or a journalist's name.

**What it needs from you**

- On file already: your profile, the release and its date, the presskit link,
  the numbers you reported to Platforms.
- It will ask once: your mailing address (the law requires it in every outreach
  footer), which outlet gets the premiere, which traction you want quoted.
- If you connected Hunter, it can look up the person behind a desk address
  (`info@`) — about one credit per outlet, after you say yes.

**Example**

> **Artist:** My EP is out November 13. Who do I pitch in Montreal, in French?
> **Agent:** Here is the Quebec list, tier 1 first: four dailies' culture desks, three specialist sites, two magazines, one culture TV show. Exclaim! assigns reviews about a month ahead, so the Canada list goes out this week. Want the master pitch first?
> **Artist:** Yes, in French. I have no press quotes yet.
> **Agent:** Done — 180 words, your voice, one ask: an interview. The quote line reads [CITATION DE PRESSE] until you have one. Next: name the one outlet you want for the premiere.
