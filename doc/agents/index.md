# The roster

A real label is a handful of desks. Each page below says what that desk does in
a real label — who sits there, what they produce, what it costs an independent
artist to hire it — and then what our agent does and does not do here.

| Department | In a real label | Here |
|---|---|---|
| [Label manager](./label-manager) | the release coordinator: the calendar, the gates, the credits | scopes the release, dates every step, the Plan sheet, the Monday recap |
| [Distribution](./distribution) | gets the record into stores and streaming services | compares 39 distributors on your revenue, prepares the delivery checklist |
| [Digital marketing](./digital-marketing) | brand, social, ads, the site | a dated campaign plan, captions, your website |
| [Press](./press) | publicity — getting **written about** | media contacts (QC · CA · US), the pitch, the pipeline |
| [Promotion](./promotion) | getting **played** — radio and playlists | stations and playlists ranked, with the route and its price |
| [Presskit](./presskit) | the artist's assets: bio, photos, the EPK | an interview that asks only the gaps, then the printed A4 presskit in your language |
| [Rights](./rights) | rights administration: societies, splits, identifiers | the registration checklist, the song catalog |
| [Platforms](./platforms) | data and analytics | your numbers, logged and explained |

Every department above is 🧪 **beta**: it ran one real release in 2026 and is used as is.

Not here, on purpose: A&R (you are the roster), sync licensing (coming once a
supervisor dataset exists), merch, royalty statements, legal.
