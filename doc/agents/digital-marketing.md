# Digital marketing

**Status:** 🧪 beta

> the desk that gives people a reason to look — the release campaign, the content, the ads, and the website every campaign lands on.

## In a real label

**Who.** A marketing department at a major; at an independent, the same desk that keeps the website (Sub Pop lists `webmaster@` beside `press@` and `radio@` — [subpop.com/contact](https://www.subpop.com/contact)). Berklee describes marketing as the artist's "long-term brand and social media presence" ([Berklee](https://www.berklee.edu/careers/roles/promotion-manager)) and the *social media manager* as the person writing the posts, making the image and video content, running "long-term digital marketing campaigns" and tracking engagement ([Berklee](https://www.berklee.edu/careers/roles/social-media-manager)).

**Week to week.** Build the campaign calendar around the street date, shoot once and cut many ways, write the captions, buy the ads, read the numbers, keep the site current. Marketing, publicity and promotion are three jobs that are routinely confused: this desk does not pitch journalists (press) or radio and playlists (promotion) — Berklee draws the same line (above).

**What they produce.** A dated content plan, the assets, the ad campaigns, and the website.

**What it costs.** Nobody publishes a freelance rate for a music social-media manager — varies, ask for a quote. What the channels themselves cost to start is published:

|---|---|---|

| TikTok Ads | campaign above $50 a day | [TikTok Ads Help](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/budget) |

| A creator post | nano creators averaged $1,105 a post in 2022 (IZEA); ~55% of them charge under $500 (Influencer Marketing Hub survey) | [IZEA](https://www.globenewswire.com/fr/news-release/2023/2/8/2604135/0/en/IZEA-Releases-The-2023-State-of-Influencer-Earnings.html) · [IMH](https://influencermarketinghub.com/influencer-marketing-benchmark-report/) |

And the money back: FACTOR's Juried Sound Recording covers 50% of eligible marketing costs up to $25,000 ([FACTOR](https://www.factor.ca/programs/artist-programs/juried-sound-recording-album/)); Musicaction's Commercialisation nationale covers 50% up to $50,000 for a release that is 70% francophone ([Musicaction](https://musicaction.ca/programmes/commercialisation-nationale/volet-1-promotion/)). The grants desk handles the application.

**Where the line is.** Press is being *written about*; promotion is being *played*; this desk is everything you publish yourself. The label manager sets the milestone ("campaign starts the 4th"); this desk turns it into the dated tasks.

## Here

The Digital Marketing Agent turns a single, EP or album into a phased campaign — one interview, then 14–20 dated tasks from about four weeks before the release to four weeks after, each with its content, its cost and its posting time — and drafts the creative in your language, with Quebec, Canada and the US as three equal markets. Every plan is built for Instagram, TikTok, Facebook and YouTube whether or not the accounts exist yet; drop one and it stays dropped. Budget arithmetic is code: an over-budget plan is scaled and told to you, a $0 plan rewrites every paid task into its organic version. Open it with `houselabel digital-marketing`.

| Skill | What you get | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Plan the campaign | The interview (title, date, budget and lead single are offered from the label manager's plan, never re-asked), a menu of about fifty content ideas you pick from, then the dated plan: tease → announce → count down → release day → keep going. Tasks on your calendar and one empty Drive folder per thing to make. | 🧪 beta |
| Draft the content | Captions in each platform's own style, two or three ad-copy variations, hashtag sets in FR and EN when you are bilingual, or 12–15 content ideas with a real first line. Approved captions can be saved as **drafts** in your Buffer; you schedule them. | ✅ developed |
| Build the website | Five skippable questions, then one bilingual page rendered from your presskit, releases, platforms and booked shows, previewed, and deployed to your own Vercel when you say so. A gap is an empty box, never an invented line. | ✅ developed |

**What it never does**

- Posts, spends, or contacts anyone. Everything is a draft until you say yes.
- Buys streams, followers or playlist placement — not as an option, not on request.
- Promises virality, editorial, press, stream counts or follower growth.
- Pitches press, radio, playlists or curators — those are the press and promotion desks.
- Reads your analytics. It cannot see a platform; the platforms desk logs the numbers you report.

**What it needs from you**

On file already: who you are (genre, city, language, career stage), the scoped project, your presskit (quotes, awards, brand colours), your releases and booked shows. It asks for: your goal as a number with a date, your weekly capacity, what footage you have, how you feel on camera, the budget, and "anything else" — the two weeks you are away is the answer that saves the plan.

**Example**

> **Artist:** Single out in five weeks, Montréal, I write in French. I have maybe $80 and I hate being on camera.
> **Agent:** $80 clears Meta — no floor there — so the plan runs $5 a day for two weeks on the one video that tests best. Not TikTok ads: those start at $50 a day. Camera off is fine; the ideas will lean on lyric-on-screen, hands-on-guitar and the room, not your face. One question: in five weeks, what number would make this a win — pre-saves, or listeners?
> **Artist:** 150 pre-saves.
> **Agent:** Noted. The pre-save link goes live at announcement, three weeks out, and I'll mark the task that gets you there. Here is the content menu — pick by number.
