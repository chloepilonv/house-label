# Presskit

**Status:** 🧪 beta

> Your bio, photos, quotes and links in one deck — the document you hand a stranger who decides something.

## In a real label

**Who does it.** Nobody has the title. The presskit comes out of publicity or
creative services; no industry body names an owner
([Berklee — Publicist](https://www.berklee.edu/careers/roles/publicist)). At an
independent label it sits behind the `press@` inbox
([Sub Pop](https://www.subpop.com/contact)). Here it is its own desk on purpose,
because every other desk uses it: press links to it, promotion attaches it,
grants and festival juries ask for it.

**What it contains.** A short bio and a long one, press and booking contacts, the
discography, the platforms, upcoming and past shows, awards, press quotes and
clippings, the team, videos and photos. Three of those — discography, socials
and streaming numbers — live elsewhere and are printed, never copied, so the
deck cannot drift from the truth.

**Who opens it, and what they open first.** The music, always. A press desk
streams the song before reading a word — "Submissions without music will not be
considered" ([Exclaim!](https://exclaim.ca/contact/getcoverage)). A festival
jury opens one live-performance video; a music video "will not be judged"
([Folk Alliance International](https://www.folk.org/conference/official-showcases)).
A showcase market wants a short bio, links and recent photos in a form, nothing
by email ([M pour Montréal](https://mpourmontreal.com/en/call-for-artists-m-for-montreal-2026/)).
Two of them refuse Dropbox and Drive links outright. The deck is the summary,
not the submission.

**What it costs.** Varies — no bio writer, photographer or designer publishes a
standard rate, and no outlet publishes a one-sheet spec. Ask for a quote. The
one spec that exists is a photo spec, and it moves: Folk Alliance asked for a
2 MB, 72 dpi headshot in 2026 and 750 px square at 300 dpi for 2027
([FAI](https://www.folk.org/conference/official-showcases)).

**The line with the next desk.** Presskit builds the document; [Press](./press)
pitches it. Your website is a separate thing: [Digital marketing](./digital-marketing)
renders one page from this presskit and puts it online.

## Here

The Presskit Agent runs a short interview — one question per turn, in French or
English — that reads what you have already written before asking anything,
saves each fact the moment you give it, then produces a printed A4 deck in your
own Google Drive as Slides and PDF. The word is "presskit", never "EPK". Open it
with `houselabel presskit`.

| Skill | What you get | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Build the presskit | the intake (your visual direction first, because the whole design comes from it), then a themed printed A4 deck: cover, biography, discography, platforms, shows, recognition, press, team, gallery | developed |

Each slide the data cannot fill reads exactly "To be completed" / « À compléter »
— never filler. Your colour choices beat the moodboard, which beats the genre
default, and every text-on-background pair clears the WCAG contrast floor
(4.5:1 for body text, [W3C](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#contrast-minimum)).

**What it never does**

- Sends or pitches the deck. It lands in your Drive; Press takes it from there.
- Quotes a streaming number you have not reported — an unreported metric is
  missing, not zero. The numbers are [Platforms](./platforms)'.
- Saves a press quote without a source, or a show read off a poster without
  your yes. Search results are a pick-list; only what you select is kept.
- Fills an empty section with invented prose.
- Builds your website.

**What it needs from you**

- On file already: your profile, releases, songs and connected platforms — it
  never asks for those again.
- It will ask: your aesthetic direction ("no preference" is an answer), the
  bios if none exist, press and booking emails, quotes with their source, three
  photos (hero, secondary, gallery), shows, awards, team.
- A direct listening and viewing link for juries and press desks — unlisted
  YouTube, SoundCloud or your site, not a Drive folder.

**Example**

> **Artist:** I need a presskit for M pour Montréal. I'm a singer-songwriter from Gatineau, two EPs out.
> **Agent:** Your two EPs and your Instagram are already on file, so I won't ask for those. First question: when you picture the deck, what does it look like — a photo, a film, a colour?
> **Artist:** Polaroids, warm, a bit faded.
> **Agent:** Saved. That gives a cream background with dark text. Next: I found a Le Droit piece from March naming your second EP — is it yours? If yes, I'll add it to the press slide with its link.
