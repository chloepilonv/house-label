# Owned audience

An address the artist owns, or a recurring payment. Every campaign books a send
at announcement and one on release day, and moves listeners toward one of the
two. Same mechanics in Quebec, Canada and the United States; every figure is
US or global.

## Email and SMS

| Metric | Figure | Source (read 2026-09-08) |
|---|---|---|
| Cross-industry email open rate | 42.35% — **inflated**: Apple Mail Privacy Protection preloads images, and Apple Mail is ~46% of clients | [Mailchimp benchmarks](https://mailchimp.com/resources/email-marketing-benchmarks/) |
| Cross-industry click rate · click-to-open | ~2.5% · ~5.3% | same |
| Independent-creator email (all niches) | 44% open · 3.7% click · 4% form opt-in | [Kit, 2024-07-24](https://kit.com/resources/blog/email-marketing-stats) |
| Instagram organic reach of followers | under 10% — practitioner; Meta publishes none | — |
| Musician-specific email or SMS benchmarks | **none first-party**; Mailchimp and Kit publish no music row | — |

- **Measure a release email by clicks to the smart link, never by opens.** Say so
  to an artist reporting a 45% open rate as a success.
- **SMS:** two sends per release — announcement and release morning — never
  more. Response-rate figures in circulation are vendor blogs and are not quoted.
- **A list of 200 outperforms a post to 2,000** is the working rule; the numbers
  above are why.

## Pre-save

| Fact | Figure | Source (read 2026-09-08) |
|---|---|---|
| **Spotify Countdown Page eligibility** | **≥3,000 monthly active listeners**; **new, original albums and EPs only — singles, reissues, remasters and compilations are not eligible**; on a multi-artist release only the first-named artist can create it; delivered via DDEX | [Spotify for Artists](https://support.spotify.com/us/artists/article/getting-started-with-countdown-pages-on-spotify/) |
| What it does | pre-saves land in the library on release day; shows on the profile, Now Playing, Search and Home; previews the tracklist; sells merch; **captures no email** | [Countdown Pages](https://artists.spotify.com/countdown-pages) |
| Published 7+ days before release | ~2× the pre-saves | same |
| Pre-savers who become "super listeners" by end of release week | nearly 1 in 7 | same |
| Album pre-savers who stream in week one | ~70% | [Spotify release guide](https://artists.spotify.com/blog/release-guide-preparing-for-release-day) |
| Pre-save rate on a smart link, Spotify · Apple Music | >30% · 24%; one-click flow moved conversion from 21% to 32% | [Linkfire](https://www.linkfire.com/blog/decoding-pre-save-rates), its own link data |

- **A single, or an artist under 3,000 listeners, pre-saves through a
  third-party link** — Linkfire, Feature.fm, ToneDen, Laylo, Hypeddit, Show.co.
  These add the email or SMS capture Spotify's page lacks. Named as products
  that exist; no independent comparison of their conversion exists. Recommend
  from `catalog.tools`.
- **If the flow asks for more than a tap, that is where the campaign loses
  people.**
- **An album's pre-save is a different link from the lead single's** — see the
  record arc in [`marketing-fundamentals.md`](./marketing-fundamentals.md).

## Fan Support

Spotify's profile link to Cash App, GoFundMe, PayPal.me or Mercado Pago; Spotify
takes no cut
([policies](https://support.spotify.com/us/artists/article/fan-support-policies/)).
Last published adoption: 200,000+ artists, 50M+ clicks, as of 2022-05-26
([Spotify](https://artists.spotify.com/blog/hello-fan-support-artist-fundraising-pick-has-a-new-name));
nothing newer exists.

## Superfans

Luminate's definition and two dated readings — the gap is real, name it:

| Reading | Share of US music listeners | What they do | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2023 mid-year | **19%** (15% of the general public) | spend 80% more per month on music; physical buyers 2.28× as likely to be superfans | [Luminate](https://luminatedata.com/blog/why-are-super-fans-so-valuable/) |
| 2024 year-end | **20%** (from 18%) | $113/month on live (+55%); 73% buy merch vs 26%; 90% attend a show vs 59% | [Luminate via MBW](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/15-of-the-general-population-of-the-us-are-superfans-heres-what-that-means-for-the-music-business1/) — report gated |

Fan-club and exclusive-content programs exist to give that fifth somewhere to put
more money; they are not a growth tactic for the other 80%. **More than half of
all merch clicks happen in the first 24 days after a release**
([Spotify release guide](https://artists.spotify.com/blog/release-guide-preparing-for-release-day)).

## Bandcamp, Patreon, Discord

| Platform | What is published | Source (read 2026-09-08) |
|---|---|---|
| Bandcamp fees | **15% on digital**, 10% once trailing-12-month sales pass US$5,000; **10% on physical**; share applies to the first $100 of any item; processor fees (~2.9% + $0.30) separate | [Bandcamp Help](https://get.bandcamp.help/en/articles/15263193-what-are-bandcamp-s-fees) |
| Bandcamp Fridays | Bandcamp waives its share; **$154M** to artists and labels since March 2020, $19M in 2025, record day $3.8M on 2025-12-05; eight dates in 2026 | [Bandcamp](https://blog.bandcamp.com) · [MBW](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/bandcamp-fridays-hit-154m-in-payouts-since-2020-with-19m-paid-in-2025-alone/) |
| Bandcamp Artist Subscriptions | recurring pledges to one artist, since 2015-09-10; whether the standard fee applies unchanged is not stated in either help article. **Bandcamp Clubs** (2025) are curator-run and are not an artist's fan club | [Bandcamp](https://blog.bandcamp.com/2015/09/10/artist-subscriptions) |
| Patreon | $10B+ paid to creators since 2013, $2B+/year, 300,000+ creators, 25M+ paid memberships — a CEO statement, reported; no musician-specific breakdown exists | [Axios, 2025-08-05](https://www.axios.com/2025/08/05/patreon-10-billion-creator-economy-ai) |

Bandcamp Fridays and fee figures are platform-wide; no source breaks them down
