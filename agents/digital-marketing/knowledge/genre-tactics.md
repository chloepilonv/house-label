ad floors do not — [`platform-conventions.md`](./platform-conventions.md) and

## The matrix

The **content register** column is house convention — no platform publishes
genre-level format performance. The **platform** column names only platforms
with a first-party or dated source below. Anchors are the institutions an
artist in that genre submits to, plays or gets covered by; dates for the
pan-genre ones (Francos, FEQ, Osheaga, Polaris, Junos, SXSW) are in
[`quebec-and-canada-market.md`](./quebec-and-canada-market.md).

| Genre | Beyond the big four | Content register | Quebec anchors | Canada anchors | US anchors |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hip-hop / rap | **SoundCloud** — the genre's launch platform; [Genius](https://genius.com/) for lyrics | punchlines as text overlays, freestyle and studio clips, features announced as events, comments answered in the artist's voice | [Gala Dynastie](https://www.placedesarts.com/en/event/gala-dynastie) (Black Quebec culture, April) · [JOAT](https://joatfestival.com/en/programming/) (hip-hop culture, September) · Francouvertes | Junos Rap categories (restructured for 2027 — [junoawards.ca](https://junoawards.ca/the-juno-awards-reshape-categories-for-2027-to-reflect-how-canadian-music-is-made-and-released-today/)) · Polaris | [SXSW](https://sxsw.com/music-festival/) · [Rolling Loud](https://www.rollingloud.com/) (festival, not a showcase) |
| Indie / alternative rock | **Bandcamp**; SoundCloud's "indie wave" is growing | live-room performance, lyric fragments, the record as an object (vinyl, artwork) | [POP Montréal](https://popmontreal.com/) (Sept 23–27, 2026) · [M pour Montréal](https://mpourmontreal.com/en/) (Nov 18–21, 2026, industry showcase) · [FME](https://www.fmeat.org/en/) Rouyn-Noranda (Sept 3–6, 2026) | Polaris · [BreakOut West](https://breakoutwest.ca/) (Sept 30–Oct 4, 2026, Victoria) | SXSW |

| Electronic / dance | **SoundCloud** (discovery) · **Beatport** (where DJs buy, and the charts they read) · **Resident Advisor** (event listings and scene press) | full sets and mix clips (`#DJset`), the build-and-drop as the hook, process clips of the session | [MUTEK](https://montreal.mutek.org/) (Aug 25–30, 2026) · [Piknic Électronik](https://piknicelectronik.com/) (Sundays, summer) · [Igloofest](https://igloofest.ca/) (winter) | Junos Electronic/Dance (renamed for 2027) · [Shambhala](https://shambhalamusicfestival.com/) and [Bass Coast](https://basscoast.ca/) (BC festivals) | [Movement](https://www.movementfestival.com/) Detroit · [Miami Music Week](https://www.miamimusicweek.com/) |
| Pop | none beyond the four; TikTok is the discovery engine (84% of Global 200 entries in 2024 went viral there first — [`marketing-fundamentals.md`](./marketing-fundamentals.md)) | one lyric line as a meme or dance, high-polish visuals, the artist as a character | Francouvertes · [Gala de l'ADISQ](https://www.adisq.com/) · Francos | Junos · Polaris | SXSW |
| R&B / soul | SoundCloud and YouTube for the vocal take | the voice alone (a cappella, harmony stacks), lit warm, slow edits | Gala Dynastie | Junos R&B/Soul | SXSW |

## What is documented per genre

### Hip-hop / rap

- SoundCloud is where the genre starts: its own report names **EsDeeKid** and
  **fakemink** as UK underground rap artists who "built early momentum" there;
  underground UK rap streams are up **more than 4.5×** in two years, and more
  than a third of that scene's tracks are tagged electronic.
  ([SoundCloud, *Music Intelligence Report 2026*](https://soundcloud.com/company/music-intelligence-report-2026)
  · [*Genre Breaking*](https://soundcloud.com/company/music-intelligence-report-2026---genre-breaking), read 2026-09-09)
- The genre line is blurring on the upload side: hip-hop-influenced indie
  uploads tagged folk/indie/rock went from **25%** (early 2024) to **34%**
  (H2 2025); 57% of Deftones listeners have hip-hop as their most-listened
  genre. (same, *Genre Breaking*)
- Biggest US genre, shrinking share: 349.9bn streams in 2025, yet the Hot 100's
  Top 40 carried **zero rap songs** on the chart dated 2025-10-25, the first
  time since February 1990; overall share ~24% in 2025 from a ~30% peak in
  2020. ([Billboard](https://www.billboard.com/music/rb-hip-hop/no-rap-songs-hot-100-top-40-first-time-since-1990-1236100625/)
  · [CNN, 2025-10-30](https://www.cnn.com/2025/10/30/entertainment/rap-falls-out-top-40))
- In Quebec, rap sits inside the 19.6% R&B/hip-hop share; the ISQ publishes no
  rap-only figure.

### Indie, alternative and folk

- Rock was the fastest-growing US genre by share in 2025 (+0.3 pt); SoundCloud's
  "indie wave" streams are up more than 4.5× in two years. (Luminate and
  SoundCloud, above)
- A Quebec label on editorial playlists for this genre: new-release lists
  ("Fresh Folk, Brand New Indie") hold a song two to three weeks then push it
  down every Friday; mood lists ("Your Favorite Coffee House", "Cosy Sunday
  Morning") are where songs stay and money is made.
  ([ADISQ/ANIM, Feb 2026, pp.47–48](https://www.adisq.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Rapport-2025-Recherche-sur-la-commercialisation-de-la-musique-francophone-sur-les-plateformes-de-musique-en-continu.pdf))
  How to reach either is promotion's.
- The dated folk case: Noah Kahan posted a verse of "Stick Season" to TikTok in
  2022, finished the song around the response, and the album debuted at No. 14
  on the Billboard 200 in October 2022.
  ([Billboard](https://www.billboard.com/music/features/noah-kahan-stick-season-breakthrough-snl-tour-grammy-preview-1235588318/)
  · [NPR, 2024-02-04](https://www.npr.org/2024/02/04/1228668717/noah-kahan-tiktok-grammys-stick-season-interview))
  The mechanism was TikTok, not a genre-specific format.

### Electronic / dance

- "The fastest growing genre for several years running" on SoundCloud; electronic
  uploads went from 1 in 4 (2020) to over 1 in 3 (2025); `#DJset` tags up 39%
  year over year; dubstep returning in the US, UK garage and minimal tech house
  crossing borders. (SoundCloud, above)
- Three platforms, three jobs: SoundCloud for discovery, [Beatport](https://www.beatport.com/top-100)
  for the purchase and the sub-genre charts DJs read, [Resident Advisor](https://ra.co/)
  for event listings and scene press — an RA listing works more like a playlist
  add than a press hit.
- The growing sub-genre changes yearly: **Afro house** downloads grew 778% in
  2025 (760,355 → 6,674,943), ~70% of all house growth; speed garage +625%.
  ([Splice × MIDiA, *Sounds of 2026*, 2026-01-14](https://www.midiaresearch.com/reports/splice-x-midia-sounds-of-2026))
  "Electronic" is never one audience; a plan names the sub-genre.

### Pop

- Two dated mechanics, not a rule: **"Espresso"** (Sabrina Carpenter, released
  2024-04-11) became a TikTok meme and dance off one line, peaked No. 4 on the
  Hot 100 and finished as 2024's most-streamed song on Spotify and Apple Music
  ([Billboard](https://www.billboard.com/music/music-news/sabrina-carpenter-reacts-espresso-top-streamed-song-2024-1235846543/));
  **Chappell Roan's** catalog rose 32% (941,000 → 1.24M streams) over the first
  weekend opening Olivia Rodrigo's tour, 2024-02-24/25
  ([Billboard, 2024-02-28](https://www.billboard.com/music/chart-beat/olivia-rodrigo-streaming-gains-guts-world-tour-chappell-roan-1235617725/)).
  Meme virality on one line, and crossover through a bigger co-sign.
- Pop is Quebec's largest genre (29%) and the one where francophone repertoire
  is most exposed to the anglophone feed — the language split is in
  [`quebec-and-canada-market.md`](./quebec-and-canada-market.md).

### R&B / soul

- Luminate counts R&B and hip-hop as one headline category; the one dated
  R&B-specific cut: a top-five share-growth quarter in Q1 2025, "for the first
  time in three years", on +9.1% volume — and one collaboration, SZA and Kendrick
  Lamar's "30 for 30" at 173.3M streams, carried much of it. A sub-genre's
  growth quarter can be one song.
  ([Luminate, 2025-04-22](https://luminatedata.com/blog/how-rb-hip-hop-streaming-share-could-get-its-groove-back/))

### Country

- Fourth US genre at 122.5bn streams; a country single, Shaboozey's "A Bar Song
  (Tipsy)", was No. 3 across all radio formats in 2025 at 2.709bn audience
  impressions — radio still makes country hits. (Luminate 2025 year-end, above)
- Francophone country has few playlists; anglophone, pan-Canadian country has
  many more, and more competition. (a Quebec label, [ADISQ/ANIM, Feb 2026, p.12](https://www.adisq.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Rapport-2025-Recherche-sur-la-commercialisation-de-la-musique-francophone-sur-les-plateformes-de-musique-en-continu.pdf))
- Musicaction's own sample of Quebec artists spans Pop, Country, Rap, RnB, Folk,
  Trad and Rock (same report, p.4); neither FACTOR nor Musicaction publishes a
  genre list — the application form asks for the genre as free text
  ([FACTOR JSR](https://www.factor.ca/programs/artist-programs/juried-sound-recording-album/)),
  and Musicaction's only genre rule relaxes the Canadian-creator requirement
  for classical, world, traditional and traditional jazz
  ([Musicaction](https://musicaction.ca/programmes/commercialisation-nationale/volet-1-promotion/)).
