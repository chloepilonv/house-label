# Marketing fundamentals

How a release is found, and the shape of the campaign that gives people a
reason to look. Quebec, Canada and the United States share every mechanic here;
what differs by market is in
[`quebec-and-canada-market.md`](./quebec-and-canada-market.md).

## How discovery happens

First-party figures only — the platform or the measurement company itself.

| Fact | Figure | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Tracks delivered to streaming services per day | **106,000** (2025, +7%); 253M tracks in catalogue | [Luminate 2025 year-end](https://luminatedata.com/reports/yearend-music-industry-report-2025), read 2026-09-08 |
| Tracks under 1,000 streams in the year | **88%**; 120.5M under 10 | same |
| Tracks taking half of all streams | 541,000 — 0.2% took 49.4% | same |
| Music time spent on video (long + short) vs audio streaming vs radio | **31% · 32% · 17%** | [IFPI Engaging with Music 2023, p.6](https://www.ifpi.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/IFPI-Engaging-With-Music-2023_full-report.pdf), 26 markets |
| 16–24s using short-form video for music daily | 82% | same |
| Songs entering Billboard Global 200 (2024) that went viral on TikTok first | **84%** | [TikTok Newsroom / Luminate, 2025-02-13](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/tiktok-and-luminate-release-latest-music-impact-report) |
| TikTok users who discover new artists there · US TikTok users paying for a streaming service | 75% · **62%** (vs 43% overall) | [TikTok Newsroom / Luminate](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/music-impact-report-confirms-tiktok-fuels-music-discovery) |
| Most-used social platform, Americans 12–34 | TikTok, 29% | [Edison Research, Infinite Dial 2026](https://www.edisonresearch.com/the-infinite-dial-2026/) |
| A release's first-year streams that come **after** the first month | **75%** | [Spotify for Artists release guide](https://artists.spotify.com/blog/release-guide-preparing-for-release-day) |
| Seconds a Reel has to hold a viewer | **3** | [Instagram for Creators](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/tips-for-improving-your-reach) |

What follows from it:

- **Short-form video is the discovery layer, not one channel among six.** Lead
  with it for anyone under a few thousand listeners: it is where attention is
  and the only channel with no paid floor
  ([`paid-promotion.md`](./paid-promotion.md)).
- **The TikTok audience is the paying audience** (62%), not the cheap top of a
  funnel.
- **Release week is the loudest moment, not the race.** Three-quarters of the
  listening comes after month one; a plan that stops on day 7 walks away from it.
- **Nobody controls virality, editorial placement, press, stream counts or
  follower growth.** A plan never promises one.
- **Four platforms are the baseline of every plan — Instagram, TikTok, Facebook
  and YouTube (Shorts implied)** — whether or not the accounts exist yet; X and
  anything else only when the artist has it.

## The dates the release imposes

Stated identically in
[`../../label-manager/knowledge/`](../../label-manager/knowledge/), which owns
the whole release sequence.

| Date | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Release day | **Friday**, 00:01 local — the Global Release Day since 2015-07-10 | [Music Week, 2015-02-26](https://www.musicweek.com/news/read/ifpi-confirms-friday-global-release-day/060991) |
| Spotify editorial pitch | Two Spotify figures: the help centre says deliver **at least 7 days** before release; the release guide says pitch **at least two weeks** before. Plan to the two weeks; 7 days is the floor. The same pitch is what puts the song in followers' Release Radar. | [Spotify for Artists help](https://support.spotify.com/us/artists/article/pitching-music-and-videos-to-playlist-editors/) · [release guide](https://artists.spotify.com/blog/release-guide-preparing-for-release-day), both read 2026-09-08 |
| Distributor delivery | **4–6 weeks** is what a label asks for; DistroKid and CD Baby recommend 3–4; review itself takes ~2 business days and a release can go live 48 hours after delivery — with nothing built around it | [ADISQ/ANIM, Feb 2026, p.12](https://www.adisq.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Rapport-2025-Recherche-sur-la-commercialisation-de-la-musique-francophone-sur-les-plateformes-de-musique-en-continu.pdf) · [CD Baby](https://support.cdbaby.com/hc/en-us/articles/360013728951-When-should-I-set-my-release-date-and-how-long-does-distribution-take) · [DistroKid](https://support.distrokid.com/hc/en-us/articles/360013649293-How-Long-Does-It-Take-for-My-Music-to-Be-Available-in-Streaming-Services) |
| Apple Music pitch | Only the distributor can pitch (Apple Music Connect, partner roles); Apple publishes no deadline | [Apple Music Connect](https://musicconnect.apple.com/support/5575-apple-music-pitch) |

**Never call a date impossible because of distribution.** Say what each week of
lead buys — the editorial pitch first, then the press window
([`../../press/knowledge/`](../../press/knowledge/)) — and what a short one costs.
Pitching itself belongs to [`../../promotion/knowledge/`](../../promotion/knowledge/).

## The arc of a single — T-28 → release → D+28

House default; compress it before you skip it.

| Window | What happens |
|---|---|
| T-28 → T-21 | Operations: delivery confirmed, pitch submitted, smart link built, profiles refreshed, asset inventory. Nothing public. |
| T-21 → T-14 | Announcement — artwork and date — and the first emotional entry into the story. Pre-save live. |
| T-14 → T-7 | Three or more hooks tested on the same song. This is where the angle is found. |
| T-7 → T-1 | Urgency: live and acoustic content, the countdown (3–4 beats, not daily), pre-save conversion. |
| Release day | One moment, not one post: Story, feed, vertical clip, pinned post, email — together, one call to action. |
| D+1 → D+7 | Fan reaction, performance and lyric-led content, reply to every comment (Instagram names the 7-day reply window as a reach factor — source above). |
| D+8 → D+21 | Depth assets: lyric video, live session, breakdown, collaborators. |
| D+22 → D+28 | Extend the story on the strongest angle; read the numbers ([`../../platforms/knowledge/`](../../platforms/knowledge/)); tease what comes next. |

- **8–20 strong pieces across the window, not daily posting** (house default).
- **The action tasks that cannot slip:** pre-save link live at announcement ·
  smart link built and every bio updated · a Canvas on **every** track (tracks
  with one are saved or playlisted **over 4× more** — Spotify release guide
  above) · YouTube Premiere scheduled if there is a video · profiles refreshed
  before the announcement. Pre-save tools and their eligibility are in
  [`owned-audience.md`](./owned-audience.md).
- **A campaign has one narrative** ("the lyric people send but never say out
  loud", "the song made from voice notes"). "New single out now" is the
  absence of one.

## The arc of a record — twelve weeks, EP and album

The unit is the **track**, not the post. A record given the single's arc is one
announcement, one release day and eight songs nobody was introduced to.

| Window | Movement | What it is for |
|---|---|---|
| T-90 → T-60 | Foundation | The era: visual identity, bio and profile art, smart link and email capture. Nothing about the tracklist. |
| T-60 → T-30 | Expansion | The waterfall singles. **The album's own pre-save goes live with the first single** — a single's pre-save saves one song of ten. |
| T-30 → T-14 | Convergence | Announcement, **tracklist reveal**, the story behind the record, a Q&A; email and SMS, not only the feed. |
| T-14 → T-1 | Alignment | Repetition on purpose: one call to action, said the same way. Countdown. |
| Release week | Activation | **A different track each day.** |
| T+7 → T+30 | Sustainment | Live and acoustic versions, visualizers, fan reactions, a mini-campaign for each track that earned one. |

- **Compressing:** cut Foundation first, shorten Expansion to one single, never
  cut Activation to one day.
- **The tracklist reveal is a free announcement and a credits moment** — EP and
  album only. Lock the tracklist first; every name revealed must be on the
  release's CREDITS, which
  [`../../label-manager/skills/manager-build-credits/SKILL.md`](../../label-manager/skills/manager-build-credits/SKILL.md)
  owns. One shape per plan: all at once · progressive (one track a day) ·
  feature reveal (collaborators named, they repost) · guess the list (an engaged
  community).
- Movements and the album-level pre-save follow
  [Sonikit's 90-day rollout](https://www.sonikit.com/blog/articles/90-day-album-rollout-strategy-how-to-build-momentum-before-and-after-an-album-release),
  a practitioner source, read 2026-09-04.

## The waterfall

| Week | Ships |
|---|---|
| 1 | Single A |
| 5 | Single B, plus A — a 2-track release |
| 9 | Single C, plus A and B |
| 13 | The full project |

Each step is a fresh Release Radar notification, pitch and content cycle. **Use
identical audio files, ISRCs and metadata throughout** or stream counts and the
TikTok/Instagram sound associations do not carry over. An artist releasing every
six weeks gets ~9 free Release Radar notifications a year. Practitioner
convention; no platform publishes it.

## Minimal viable campaign

When budget, time, footage or camera comfort are limited — a real plan at this
size, not a worse version of a bigger one:

```
T-14        delivery and pitch check · smart link · cover and date announcement
T-10        first hook-led vertical video
T-7         the story behind the song, or a lyric-led video
T-3         live / acoustic / performance video
Release day core video · Stories · the link · profile updates · email if there is one
D+2 → D+5   two alternate short-form posts
D+7 → D+10  lyric video, visualizer or stripped performance
Week 2      a fan prompt plus one behind-the-scenes or process post
Week 3      results review, second wave on the strongest angle
```

## Ten campaign mechanics at indie scale

Major-label campaigns reduced to the mechanic that did the work. Never cite
their results; the lesson is the shape
([BIMM](https://www.bimm.ac.uk/blog/10-music-marketing-campaigns-that-changed-the-industry/),
read 2026-09-02). Pick **one** per campaign, matched to the artist, not the
release.

| Mechanic | Example | At indie scale | Needs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Synchronised drop | Beyoncé, *Lemonade* | video, smart link, newsletter, pinned post and a live all inside one hour | coordination only |
| Countdown that reveals | BTS, *Dynamite* | each beat reveals something real — a lyric, a credit, a corner of the artwork; on a record, the tracklist one track at a time | nothing |
| Interactive artefact | The Weeknd, *After Hours* | a poll that decides the B-side; a form whose answers go in the liner notes | nothing |
| Reproducible visual language | Charli XCX, *Brat* | one colour, one typeface, no exceptions, plus a template fans can fill — why brand colours go into briefs by hex | a fixed identity |
| Bridging two audiences | Addison Rae, *Addison* | raw phone cut on TikTok, composed cut on Instagram, long cut on YouTube | camera presence |
| Puzzle trail | Taylor Swift, *Taylor's Version* | one recurring detail across every asset that pays off on release day | an audience already paying attention |
| Strategic silence | Radiohead, *Kid A* | no singles, one strange recurring image, then the record | an existing audience; never an excuse for no plan |
| Gamified with a real prize | Ariana Grande, *Sweetener* | five lyric posters in five real places in one city, each with a code | printing, a local scene |
| Choose-your-own-adventure | Avicii, *Stories* | an Instagram Story sequence where one path ends at the unreleased track | nothing |
| DIY intimacy | Olivia Rodrigo, *Sour* | handwritten lyrics, the phone photo, the take with the mistake — proximity over polish | camera presence |

## The checklist a plan passes

1. It reflects **this** artist — their assets, genre, career stage and language.
2. Every task has a purpose, an owner and a date the release can bear.
3. Platform setup and the action tasks happen **before** release day.
4. Release day has one listener action; at least three story angles get tested.
5. Captions and subtitles are specified; nothing is promised, deceptive or
   rights-infringing; every post, spend and submission waits for the artist's
   yes.

When the release date, focus track, goal, platforms, assets or capacity are
missing, ask — or build a provisional plan with every assumption labelled.
