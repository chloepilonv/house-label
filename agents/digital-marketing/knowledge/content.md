# Content

What a campaign posts, and how it is made without filming every week. When and
why is [`marketing-fundamentals.md`](./marketing-fundamentals.md); per-platform
shape is [`platform-conventions.md`](./platform-conventions.md). Everything
here is practitioner convention unless a source is named.

## The eight categories

A plan draws from most of them. One that draws from two is a plan of
promotional posts, and the audience stops looking.

| Category | What it is | Costs a shoot? |
|---|---|---|
| Behind the scenes | studio, writing, rehearsal, gear, the room it was made in | yes |
| Storytelling | what the song is about, a lyric broken down, the thing that happened | no — face or text |
| Fan engagement | a question, a poll, a duet or stitch prompt, a listening party | no |
| Visual | artwork reveal, tracklist reveal (EP/album only), moodboard, visualizer, lyric video | design |
| Milestones | a stream count, a playlist add, a grant, a first — only real ones | no |
| Collaboration | features, the producer, the band, another local artist | shared |
| Educational | how something was made, a piece of the process nobody sees | often reuses footage |
| Promotional | pre-save, the link, the date, the merch — the smallest share | no |

**Two or three low-effort ideas per plan** — a text post, a poll, a repost of
something that exists — and never more create tasks than the artist's weekly
capacity carries. A plan made entirely of shoots is abandoned in week two.

## Hooks and the 3-second rule

Instagram's own guidance: "make sure the first 3 seconds of your reel are
engaging"
([Instagram for Creators](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/tips-for-improving-your-reach),
read 2026-09-08). No other platform publishes a number.

**Visual hook + verbal hook + text overlay, all three in the first three
seconds.** Motion or a face, never a static wide; the most arresting line, which
is almost never the first line; on-screen text so it works on mute.

| Hook type | Formula | For a release |
|---|---|---|
| Curiosity | "Nobody tells you [thing]" | "Nobody tells you the demo is usually better" |
| Story | "The day [event]" | "The day the string broke, take nine" |
| Value | "How I [outcome]" | "How I recorded this in a kitchen" |
| Contrarian | "[Thing] is overrated" | "Perfect vocals are overrated. This take has a crack in it." |
| Lyric-first | the line itself, on screen, cold | the line people repeat — not the title |

**Test at least three hooks on the same audio** in the T-14 → T-7 window. Same
song, different opening; the audience picks the story before release day depends
on it. Instagram's **Trial Reels** show a post to non-followers first — built for
this.

## One shoot → a month of posts

An asset is a source of self-contained moments, not a post. **A long video and
its short cut are two create tasks** with two post groups.

| From a live-session shoot | Format | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Full session | long video | YouTube |
| One vertical performance clip per song | short video | Instagram · TikTok · Shorts |
| Best 15 seconds, text-overlaid | short video | Instagram · TikTok · Shorts |
| Silent cut with burned captions | short video | Facebook feed |
| Setup and soundcheck | short video | behind the scenes |
| Stills pulled from the video | carousel | Instagram |
| Lyric over the performance footage | short video | all vertical |

One create task, seven post tasks. Other sources: **a studio day** — demo vs
final, the vocal stack soloed, the take that failed, the notebook; **the
artwork** — reveal, rejects, a detail crop with the lyric, the physical copy in
hand; **a press feature** — the pull quote as a card, the artist reading it, a
Story screenshot with the link. **Target: 5–7 post tasks per shoot.** One means
the shoot was planned as a post.

**Repost nothing verbatim.** Instagram says it is "less likely to recommend
reposts of a reel that's already on Instagram" and names Remix as the
alternative (same source). Mechanics of Remix, Duet, Stitch and Collabs are in
[`platform-conventions.md`](./platform-conventions.md).

## Batching

One session a week, two to three hours, in this order: review last week's
numbers (15 min) · pick the angles that earned another go (15) · write every
hook and caption (45) · cut every clip (60) · schedule everything (15). Cutting
one clip a day is the pattern that gets abandoned. Reading the numbers is
[`../../platforms/knowledge/`](../../platforms/knowledge/)'s job.

**Before writing hooks for a new artist:** look at the last 20 posts of 5–10
artists one tier above them, note the outliers, write down the hook type, format
and first frame of each, use the pattern — never the post.

## Surfaces that need no shoot

Free, permanent, and usually forgotten. Each is one action task.

- **The smart link** — one URL every post points at, every service plus the
  pre-save. A bio link still pointing at last year's single spends attention on
  the wrong record.
- **Spotify for Artists** — Artist Pick on the pre-save page then the release; a
  Canvas on **every** track (an eight-song EP is eight uploads); Clips and
  music videos uploaded; profile image, header and bio matching the artwork.
- **Genius** — lyrics uploaded and annotated: storytelling content that also
  lands in search and Spotify's lyric panel.
- **YouTube** — chapters, end screens, a pinned comment, the smart link in the
  description; a Premiere instead of a plain upload when there is a video.
- **Email and SMS** — one send at announcement, one on release morning
  ([`owned-audience.md`](./owned-audience.md)).

## The task title says what the thing is

A title is read alone, in a list, weeks after the conversation. Real artists
came back with "what is that?" — so every title names the platform and, where
the thing is not self-evident, what it is.

| Not this | This |
|---|---|
| Canvas for "Song" | **Upload the Spotify Canvas for "Song"** — the 3–8 s loop behind the song |
| Countdown Page live | **Publish the Spotify Countdown Page** — the pre-save page with the tracklist and countdown |
| Smart link built | **Build the smart link** — one URL that opens the song in whichever app they use |
| Pitch editorial | **Pitch the single to Spotify's playlist editors** |
| Artist Pick set | **Set your Artist Pick** — the pinned item at the top of your Spotify profile |
| EPK updated | **Update the presskit** — bio, photos, a private listening link |

The brief carries the detail; the title carries enough to act without opening
anything.
