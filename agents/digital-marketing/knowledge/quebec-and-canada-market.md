## The language of the feed

|---|---|---|

| Quebec — radio listeners who prefer French songs | **56%**, against 32% of non-radio listeners | [ISQ via Radio-Canada, 2025-08-27](https://ici.radio-canada.ca/rci/fr/nouvelle/2188477/radio-musique-francophone-quebecoise-plateforme-numerique) |
| Quebec — population whose first official language spoken is French | 82.2%; 93.7% can hold a conversation in French | [Statistics Canada, 2021 Census](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/daily-quotidien/220817/dq220817a-eng.htm) |
| Canada — top 10,000 tracks by language | **95.01% anglophone**, francophone "moins de 1 %"; the remaining slices (3.74%, 0.72%, 0.54%) are instrumental and other and the report does not label which is which | [ADISQ/ANIM, Feb 2026, p.19](https://www.adisq.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Rapport-2025-Recherche-sur-la-commercialisation-de-la-musique-francophone-sur-les-plateformes-de-musique-en-continu.pdf) |
| United States — francophone share | nobody publishes one; the second-language reference is Latin at 120.9bn streams (+5.2%) in 2025 | [Luminate 2025 year-end](https://luminatedata.com/reports/yearend-music-industry-report-2025) |

- **Quebec is a French place with an English streaming feed.** A francophone
  release competing on algorithmic surfaces alone competes for a tenth of the
  plays; radio and Quebec media are where French repertoire is preferred —
  [`../../promotion/knowledge/radio-pitch.md`](../../promotion/knowledge/radio-pitch.md),
  [`../../press/knowledge/`](../../press/knowledge/).
- **In the rest of Canada the francophone feed is under 1%.** A French release
  outside Quebec is a Quebec release plus the francophone minority communities
  (Franco-Ontarian, Acadian, Western francophone), not a national one.
- **Playlists in Quebec:** of Quebec songs added to an editorial playlist, 30.99%
  showed a significant stream effect, against 28.45% for algorithmic (270 artists,
  2,624 songs — [Numéa for ADISQ, 2024-11-29](https://www.adisq.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Numea_ADISQ-Projet-decouvrabilite.pdf));
  labels describe editorial as a fast, short spike and algorithmic as slower and
  more durable ([ADISQ/ANIM, Feb 2026, p.47](https://www.adisq.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Rapport-2025-Recherche-sur-la-commercialisation-de-la-musique-francophone-sur-les-plateformes-de-musique-en-continu.pdf)).
  Reaching either is promotion's: [`../../promotion/knowledge/playlist-pitch.md`](../../promotion/knowledge/playlist-pitch.md).

## Seasonality — measured, Quebec only

Numéa modelled two years of Luminate data (Prophet) for
[ADISQ/ANIM, Feb 2026, pp.22–27](https://www.adisq.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Rapport-2025-Recherche-sur-la-commercialisation-de-la-musique-francophone-sur-les-plateformes-de-musique-en-continu.pdf):

| Moment | Effect | Plan on it when |
|---|---|---|
| Saint-Jean (24 June) | clear spike for Québécois francophone catalogue; weaker for francophone-minority artists; "très modeste" for anglo-Quebec and international tracks | the repertoire is francophone |
| Holidays | Christmas songs climb from late November, peak late December; labels release them **early November** because the holiday playlists are built then | there is a holiday song |
| Saint-Valentin | no aggregate movement; some tracks rise | never as a campaign anchor |

No equivalent study exists for the rest of Canada or the US.

## Marketing money — the two figures a plan needs

Everything else about a program — eligibility, intakes, deadlines — is on
the funder's own page; check its calendar before planning around it.

| Program | Covers | Rule that changes the plan | Source (read 2026-09-09) |
|---|---|---|---|
| FACTOR, Juried Sound Recording — marketing component | **50%** of eligible marketing expenses, up to **$25,000** | English-market projects | [FACTOR](https://www.factor.ca/programs/artist-programs/juried-sound-recording-album/) |
| Musicaction, Commercialisation nationale, volet 1 — promotion | **50%** of accepted costs, up to **$50,000** per project | **70% francophone content** — an English-majority release does not qualify; decided at intake, not at application | [Musicaction](https://musicaction.ca/programmes/commercialisation-nationale/volet-1-promotion/) |

**Streamers are not funding FACTOR or Musicaction.** The CRTC ordered a 5% base
contribution from streaming groups over $25M in Canadian revenue, 2% of it to
FACTOR and Musicaction on a 60–40 English/French split
([CRTC 2024-121, 2024-06-04](https://crtc.gc.ca/eng/archive/2024/2024-121.htm)).
Payments were stayed in December 2024; a Department of Justice letter of
2026-07-17 states the government will eliminate the requirement
([Michael Geist, 2026-07-29](https://www.michaelgeist.ca/2026/07/starting-over-court-filing-confirms-the-crtcs-streamer-contribution-decisions-are-dead-with-a-full-online-streaming-act-reset-to-come/));
on 2026-06-03 the government announced $600M a year for music and media, $200M
of it replacing the base contributions to FACTOR, Musicaction, Starmaker,
RadioStar and others ([Billboard Canada, 2026-06-03](https://ca.billboard.com/business/streaming/canadian-government-music-funding-600-million)).
Never tell an artist streamers "must fund" a program; the money is public and
its rate is not yet on the record.

## Meta blocks Canadian news links

Since 2023-08-01 Meta blocks links and content from Canadian news outlets on
Facebook and Instagram for users in Canada, in response to the Online News Act
([Meta Newsroom](https://about.fb.com/news/2023/06/changes-to-news-availability-on-our-platforms-in-canada/));
the block is still in place and most Canadian users do not know it
([National Observer, 2026-07-15](https://www.nationalobserver.com/2026/07/15/news/canadians-meta-news-block)).
For a campaign: a review in La Presse, Le Devoir, CBC or Exclaim! cannot be
shared as a link on Instagram or Facebook in Canada — post the quote as a
graphic or a screenshot, and put the link on the website, the newsletter, X or
TikTok. Sharing in the US works.

## Regional hashtags

House convention; no public dataset gives search volume for any of these.
Taxonomy and per-platform counts are in
[`platform-conventions.md`](./platform-conventions.md).

- **Quebec, francophone** — `#MusiqueQuebecoise`, `#MusiqueFrancophone`,
  `#ArtisteFrancophone`, `#MusiqueIndependante`, `#NouvelleMusique`,
  `#HipHopQuebecois`, `#MontrealMusic`, `#QuebecMusic`.
- **Canada** — `#CanadianMusic`, `#MusiqueCanadienne`, plus the city
  (`#TorontoMusic`, `#VancouverMusic`).
- **United States** — the city and the genre (`#NYCMusic`, `#LAMusic`,
  `#ChicagoRap`).
- No `#SOCAN`, `#FACTOR` or funder tag: neither organisation asks for one, and
  FACTOR's funded-projects page requires no credit tag
  ([FACTOR](https://www.factor.ca/factor/factor-funded/)).
