# Collaboration rights and rates

Which society collects each right, and the identifiers, are in
[`rights-fundamentals.md`](./rights-fundamentals.md). This file is the two
questions that come before registration: *who else owns a piece of this song*,
and *what do I owe the people I hired*. It names the missing agreement; drafting
one is a lawyer's job.

## Who acquires what, by contributing

Rights arise from **contribution**, not from credit, payment or thanks. "We
never wrote anything down" means the split was never *recorded*, not that it
does not exist (Menon, *L'autoproduction — guide pratique et juridique*, APCM,
2025, §3.2 — cited by section, never reproduced).

| Contribution | Composition (*auteur*) | Master (*enregistrement*) | Customary money |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lyrics or music | ✅ writer share | — | performance + mechanical through the PRO, split per [`rights-fundamentals.md`](./rights-fundamentals.md#1-auteur-compositeur-writer-and-composer) |
| Arrangement | ✅ taken out of the composer's half (e.g. 5%) | — | same, on the arranger's share |
| Performance — lead, backing, session | only if they also wrote | ✅ performer's right in the fixed performance | session fee (below) + neighbouring rights as featured or non-featured |
| Producer (*réalisateur·rice*) | only if they changed words or music | ✅ works on the recording | **producer points** (below); a co-writing share on top if they co-wrote |
| Mix engineer | — | ✅ | fee; **1 point** when points are given at all (practitioner claim) |
| Mastering engineer | — | ✅ | fee; points are rare enough that no source quotes a figure |
| Photo, artwork, video | separate copyright in the image or film, owned by its maker | — | a written grant covering the intended uses |

- **"Only if" is the expensive column.** A producer who rewrote a verse in the
  booth is a writer; the artist finds out when money is already flowing on the
  old split. Ask *what did they change*, not *what was their title*.
- **Paying for the session makes you the maker of the recording** (Copyright
  Act, s 2, in `rights-fundamentals.md`). It acquires nobody else's right in
  their performance — that takes the engagement agreement below.

## Splits — convention and law

### Composition and master, the two 100% pies

- Writer shares total 100%; master splits total 100%; the two never mix.
- **No society publishes a default split.** The 50/50 *auteur*/*compositeur*
  and the publisher's cut are convention, stated in
  [`rights-fundamentals.md`](./rights-fundamentals.md#1-auteur-compositeur-writer-and-composer).
  The "artist 50 / producer 20 / label 30" master split that circulates on
  blogs has no citable source; nobody publishes one.

### Canada — a band is a partnership without signing anything

| Province | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Quebec | a contract of partnership is one where the parties agree, in a spirit of collaboration, to carry on an activity, contribute to it and share the pecuniary profits | [Code civil du Québec, art 2186](https://www.legisquebec.gouv.qc.ca/en/document/cs/CCQ-1991), read 2026-09-02 |
| Ontario | the relation between persons carrying on a business in common with a view to profit | [Partnerships Act, RSO 1990, c P.5, s 2](https://www.ontario.ca/laws/statute/90p05), read 2026-09-02 |
| other provinces | each has its own Partnership Act on the same model; only these two were read | — |

The default rules apply from the first shared show, not from the day something
is signed. A band agreement does not create obligations; it replaces the ones
the province chose. **An assignment of copyright is invalid unless written** —
"the label owns the masters" said out loud moves nothing (Menon §IV).

### United States — four rules, two of which invert what a Canadian expects

| Rule | What it means for the artist | Statute (read 2026-09-03) |
|---|---|---|
| Partnership is state law, formed by conduct | "the association of two or more persons to carry on as co-owners a business for profit forms a partnership, whether or not the persons intend to" — the Revised Uniform Partnership Act wording most states adopt | e.g. [RCW 25.05.055](https://app.leg.wa.gov/rcw/default.aspx?cite=25.05.055), Washington, read 2026-09-09 |
| A transfer must be written and signed | same as Canada; a verbal grant moves nothing | [17 U.S.C. § 204(a)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/17/204) |
| **"Work made for hire" does not cover a sound recording** | only an employee's work, or a commissioned work in one of nine listed categories with a signed writing, is made for hire — a sound recording and a song are on neither list. A session player needs an **assignment** under § 204; a "work for hire" clause alone acquires nothing. Where it does apply, the employer *is* the author | [17 U.S.C. § 101](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/17/101), [§ 201(b)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/17/201) |
| **Any co-writer can license the whole song alone** | joint authors are co-owners; each may grant a non-exclusive licence without the others' consent, owing them an accounting. A 90% share is not a veto — write "who may clear a sync" into the co-writing agreement | [17 U.S.C. § 201(a)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/17/201) |
| Termination after 35 years | an author may terminate a grant during a five-year window opening 35 years after execution; notice 2–10 years ahead, recorded with the Copyright Office. **Record the execution date of everything signed.** Whether an assigned recording is reachable turns on the work-for-hire question above — a recording that was never validly made for hire is a grant, and grants terminate | [17 U.S.C. § 203](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/17/203) |

### Photos, artwork and video — both countries

- **Canada:** the photographer is first owner of the photo; the subject holds
  a separate right in their own image; director, dancers, editor and
  choreographer all hold rights in a video (Menon §3.2).
- **US:** "the author and initial copyright owner of a photograph is the person
  who 'shoots' or 'takes' the photo"; a commissioned photo is made for hire
  only as one of the nine categories (a *contribution to a collective work*)
  with a signed writing — unlike a recording, it *can* be
  ([copyright.gov/engage/photographers](https://www.copyright.gov/engage/photographers/),
  read 2026-09-09).
- A presskit, Bandcamp page or poster needs a written grant from the
  photographer covering those uses. The presskit desk asks; the rights fact
  lives here.

### The agreements a self-producing artist needs (Menon §IV)

| When | Agreement | The question it settles |
|---|---|---|
| two or more people write | co-writing agreement | shares, who administers, who may clear a sync |
| a group exists | band agreement | who leaves with what, who keeps the name |
| someone performs on the record | engagement agreement | what the performer grants the maker, for what fee |
| a producer is hired | producer agreement | the points, off what, and whether they also wrote |
| studio, mix, mastering | engagement agreement | that their work carries no claim on the recording |

## What you owe a hired performer

Collective-agreement **minimums**, not quotes. Paying more is allowed; every
agreement says so. Three things decide which one applies: **where the session
is** (Quebec has its own statute on the status of artists; the rest of Canada
is CFM; the US is AFM or SAG-AFTRA), **which union the performer belongs to**,
and **recording or stage** — separate agreements entirely; this file covers
sound recording only.

### Quebec — UDA · GMMQ · ADISQ, accompanying artists, 2026-04-13 → 2029-04-12

Source: *Entente collective UDA–GMMQ–ADISQ, enregistrement sonore, volet
artistes accompagnateurs, 2026–2029*, ch. 8-1.03, 8-1.05, 5-2.02, 5-2.03 (read
2026-09-03; listed at [adisq.com](https://www.adisq.com/documentation/ententes-collectives/),
read 2026-09-09).

| Session | Fee | Call | Music | Each extra 5 min | Extra song |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1–9 accompanying artists | **350,00 $** (353,50 $ from 2027-04-13) | 3 h incl. a 10-min break | ≤15 min, ≤4 works | 80,00 $ | 80,00 $ |
| 10+ recording together | **275,00 $** | 3 h incl. a 10-min break | ≤20 min, ≤4 works | 75,00 $ | 80,00 $ |

- Rehearsal outside a session: 30,00 $/h, four consecutive hours minimum per
  call (30,30 $ 2027, 30,60 $ 2028). Preproduction is paid at that rate; if any
  of it ends up on the record, a per-song fee is owed on top. Score preparation
  outside working hours: rehearsal rate, two hours minimum.
- **Withheld and remitted by the producer, on top of the fee:**

| | UDA | GMMQ |
|---|---|---|
| union dues | 2,5 % | 4,5 % |
| security fund (CSA) | 2 % withheld, plus the producer's own contribution | 11 % |
| base | actual fees and royalties, **no cap** | fees and royalties **capped at twice the tariff** |

- The exact split between what is withheld from the musician and what the
  producer adds is in ch. 5-2.03; read it before quoting a budget line.
- **The featured artist's own agreements are archived** — UDA–ADISQ (disques)
  1997–2000, GMMQ–ADISQ 1996–1998 ([adisq.com](https://www.adisq.com/documentation/ententes-collectives/),
  2026-09-09). Nothing current sets what the *vedette* is owed; ask UDA or the
  GMMQ.

### Canada outside Quebec — CFM, rates from 2025-02-04

Source: [CFM fee summary chart, AFM/SRLA](https://cfmusicians.afm.org/uploads/file/SRLA%20-%20Canadian%20Summary%20Chart%202023_CSrev.pdf),
read 2026-09-09.

| Session | Side musician | Terms |
|---|---|---|
| **Regular** | **$488.29** | 3-h minimum call, 15 min of finished music |
| Special | $322.29 | only when told at booking; 1½-h minimum, 7½ min of music |
| **Low budget** | **$274.32** | 3-h minimum, 15 min of music |
| Limited pressing (non-symphonic) | $100.00 | 2-h minimum, 15 min of music; ≤3,000 units or 10,000 downloads, then upgrade to the rate above |
| Overtime | $162.77 / ½ h · $81.38 / ¼ h | ¼-h units only to finish music from the same session |

- Leader: side rate +100% (limited pressing: +50%). Pension: **11.75 %** of
  scale. Health & welfare: $30.00 first session, $25.00 each further session the
  same day. Doubling: +20 % first, +15 % each further.
- **Say the low-budget and limited-pressing tiers exist.** An independent
  release that does not know them overpays or works off-agreement.

### United States — AFM SRLA, and SAG-AFTRA for singers

- **The SRLA expired 2026-07-31.** Term 2023-03-13 → 2026-01-31, extended to
  2026-07-31 ([AFM signatory packet](https://www.afm.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/SRLA-Signatory-Packet-rates-through-7.31.2026.pdf),
  revised 2026-03-03, read 2026-09-09). As of 2026-09-09 afm.org publishes no
  successor; the last published scales are the floor to quote, dated.
- Last scales ([AFM SRLA scales 2025](https://www.afm.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/scales-full-srla-2025.pdf),
  read 2026-09-03): side musician basic session **$488.29** (3-h call, 15 min
  of music), leader **$976.58**, pension **14.09 %** of gross scale, health &
  welfare $30.00 first session / $25.00 each additional per day. The US and
  Canadian side rates are the same number; the pension percentages are not.
- **Singers are SAG-AFTRA, not AFM.** The *Sound Recordings Code* (2024, runs
  to 2026) sets per-side session rates — solo/duo $306 (2025), $316 (2026);
  groups of 3–8 $143.75 (2026) — per its
  [2021–2026 rate summary](https://www.sagaftra.org/sites/default/files/SR%20Rates%20Summary%202021-2026.pdf).
  sagaftra.org returns 403 to this repo's fetcher; figures read from the
  search excerpt 2026-09-09, confirm on the PDF before budgeting.

## Producer points, off what

- **One point = 1 % of the master's royalty**, never of the composition. A
  producer who also wrote takes a writer share separately.
- **Major-label convention: 3–5 points** (3 for a developing producer, 4–5 for
  a name, above 5 for a star), deducted from the *artist's* royalty and paid
  once recording costs are recouped
  ([Ari's Take, producer splits](https://aristake.com/producer-splits/),
  practitioner, read 2026-09-09).
- **Independent convention: 15–25 % of net master royalties, or a flat fee and
  no points** — "net" meaning after recording costs, the producer's fee,
  distribution and manufacturing (same source). The figure is the major-label
  points divided by an artist's typical royalty rate; it is not a rule.
- **Mixer: 1 point when any**, recoupable or not against the mix fee;
  mastering: none — practitioner and vendor claims
  ([majormixing.com](https://majormixing.com/what-royalty-rate-do-mixing-and-mastering-engineers-get/),
  tier 5, read 2026-09-09). No union or society publishes a producer or mixer
  rate.
- The department gives the convention with its source and never says what the
  artist should agree to.

## Split-sheet hygiene

1. **One sheet per song, before release.** A split agreed after the song earns
   is a renegotiation.
2. **Both pies on it:** writers with role, IPI and share; master splits with
   role and share. Each side totals 100 %; a 90 % total is a warning to fix,
   never a reason to block the save.
3. **Every performer with an IPN, every writer with an IPI** — a foreign
   society matches money by those numbers
   ([`rights-fundamentals.md`](./rights-fundamentals.md#identification-numbers)).
4. **Signed, dated, kept.** The execution date starts the US termination
   clock; the signature is what makes an assignment valid on both sides of
   the border.
5. **Sync clearance named** — who may license the song and the master, per
   side, since a US co-writer otherwise may alone.
