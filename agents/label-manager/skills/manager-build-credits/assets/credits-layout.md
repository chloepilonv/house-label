## Copyright lines

These are two different owners and stores need both. `℗` is the sound
recording (the master). `©` is the song (the composition).

```
℗ YYYY MASTER OWNER
© YYYY COMPOSITION OWNER / PUBLISHER
```

## Funders and acknowledgements

Grant programs require exact wording, set by the funder, and an incorrect or
missing line can affect the claim. Copy the wording from the funding
agreement — do not paraphrase it.

```
FUNDER ACKNOWLEDGEMENT LINE, VERBATIM FROM THE AGREEMENT
```

## Role vocabulary

Use these role names, not free text — they are the DDEX contributor roles
the distributor's upload form offers, so a credit written this way is
retyped rather than reinterpreted.

- **Composition:** composer · lyricist · composer-lyricist · arranger ·
  adapter · translator · publisher · administrator
- **Performance:** main artist · featured artist · non-featured performer ·
  background vocalist · conductor · orchestra · choir
- **Production:** producer · co-producer · additional production ·
  vocal producer · recording engineer · assistant engineer · mixing engineer ·
  mastering engineer · programmer · editor
- **Visual:** cover artwork · photography · art direction · design · layout
