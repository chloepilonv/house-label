# Label fundamentals

## What a label does for an artist

- A record label is "a multidepartmental company that promises to handle the
  business aspects of music so that artists can focus on their craft"
  ([Berklee](https://www.berklee.edu/careers/communities/record-industry), 2026-09-09).
- It pays first and collects later: labels "injected a total of US$8.1 billion
  globally … into A&R and marketing in 2024 alone, representing 30% of their
  revenues" ([IFPI, Music in the EU 2025](https://www.ifpi.org/record-labels-drive-growth-across-the-eu-and-urge-policymakers-to-guard-creativity-in-ai-era/), 2026-09-09).
- The pool it draws from: recorded music earned US$31.7 billion in 2025;
  streaming was 69.6% of it ([IFPI Global Music Report 2026](https://www.ifpi.org/global-music-report-2026-global-recorded-music-revenues-grow-6-4-as-record-companies-drive-innovation/), 2026-09-09).
- "Label" names the product, not the boundary. Clearing one song puts a music
  supervisor in front of "legal, label, talent management, agency, business
  management" ([Guild of Music Supervisors](https://www.guildofmusicsupervisors.com/what-is-a-music-supervisor), 2026-08-14)
  — five companies around one artist. Live is "a close cousin to the record
  industry" and a different one ([Berklee](https://www.berklee.edu/careers/communities/live-music), 2026-08-14).

## The functions, and who does each

"Large record labels … typically have marketing, publicity, promotions, legal,
and A&R departments" ([Berklee](https://www.berklee.edu/careers/communities/record-industry), 2026-09-09).
An independent publishes the same seams, shorter: Sub Pop's front doors are
General, Online Sales, Press, Radio, Licensing, Direct Sales to Stores (US
only) and Webmaster ([subpop.com/contact](https://www.subpop.com/contact), 2026-09-09).
What an indie omits is the finding — no A&R, booking, funding or analytics
inbox.

| Function | What it is | Major | Independent | Self-releasing artist — our desk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Label management** | "the central organizational hub, directly managing and synchronizing all core departments" ([Berklee](https://www.berklee.edu/careers/roles/label-manager), 2026-09-09) — the calendar and the budget | a senior post over the departments | the owner | [`label-manager`](../../agent-recruitor/knowledge/label-departments.md) — plans, never does |
| **A&R** | finding, signing, developing artists ([Berklee](https://www.berklee.edu/careers/roles/ar-representative), 2026-08-14) | a named department | the owner's ear | nobody — the artist is the roster |
| **Production** | recording, mixing, mastering, artwork | budgeted and recouped | budgeted, often profit-shared | the artist and their engineer — [release-path § Creation](./release-path.md#1-creation) |
| **Rights administration** | splits, registrations, licences, ℗ and © | business affairs and legal, plus a publisher for the song | the label manager, plus a publisher or administrator | [`rights`](../../rights/knowledge/rights-fundamentals.md) |
| **Manufacturing** | vinyl, CD, merch | an operations desk and a plant | a plant, quoted per run | the artist sources the plant |
| **Distribution** | getting the record into stores and onto the platforms | the major's own distribution company | a distributor; Sub Pop still sells direct to US stores | [`distribution`](../../distribution/knowledge/distribution-fundamentals.md) |
| **Marketing** | "artists' long-term brand and social media presence" ([Berklee](https://www.berklee.edu/careers/roles/promotion-manager), 2026-09-09) | a department | the same desk keeps the website (`webmaster@`) | [`digital-marketing`](../../digital-marketing/knowledge/marketing-fundamentals.md) |
| **Publicity** | "television appearances and magazine articles" — getting *written about* | a department, or a PR firm ([Berklee](https://www.berklee.edu/careers/roles/publicist), 2026-08-14) | `press@` | [`press`](../../press/knowledge/press-fundamentals.md) |
| **Promotion** | pitching "new tracks, records, and artists to radio stations … securing radio airplay" — getting *played*; playlists are the same desk's streaming-era work | a department | `radio@`, or bought: promoters work in "label promotion departments, independent promotion companies" or freelance ([Berklee](https://www.berklee.edu/careers/roles/radio-promoter), 2026-09-09) | [`promotion`](../../promotion/knowledge/radio-pitch.md) |
| **Licensing / sync** | master-use and synchronization licences | the label for the master; the publisher for the song | `licensing@` | nobody yet |
| **Booking** | shows, tours, festivals | not label work — an agency | not listed at Sub Pop | nobody yet |
| **Funding** | public and industry money | — | Canada: FACTOR's Company Programs exist for "record labels, managers, music publishers, and promoters" ([factor.ca](https://www.factor.ca/), 2026-08-14) | nobody yet — each funder's own page |
| **Data** | streams, sales, audience over time | inside marketing | the owner's dashboard | [`platforms`](../../platforms/knowledge/reading-a-trend.md) |
| **Presskit** | bio, photos, one-sheet | publicity or creative services — no industry body names the owner | `press@` | [`presskit`](../../presskit/knowledge/presskit-fundamentals.md) |

- **Promotion, publicity and marketing are three jobs.** Radio is promotion's
  alone; the three are routinely confused ([Berklee](https://www.berklee.edu/careers/roles/promotion-manager), 2026-09-09).
- **Headcount is not sourceable.** A2IM speaks for "over 600 independently
  owned record labels" of "all sizes and staffing levels"
  ([a2im.org](https://a2im.org/about/), 2026-09-09); "one person wears four
  hats" is plausible and uncited.

## Being your own label

- **Definition.** You own the master (the ℗ line carries your name) and you
  do, or buy, every function in the table. A distributor delivers; it does not
  own. FACTOR's test: the applicant is "the party that controls the Canadian
  commercial release rights and exclusive Canadian exploitation rights to the
  sound recording" — "a Canadian artist, if the artist is 'unsigned' or has
  not licensed the sound recording to another party"
  ([FACTOR JSR Album guidelines](https://factorportalprod.blob.core.windows.net/portal/Documents/Updates/FACTOR_Juried_Sound_Recording_Album_Program_Guidelines.pdf), 2026-09-09).
- **What you take on.** The maker side of neighbouring rights (Panorama or
  Re:Sound direct in Canada, SoundExchange's rights-owner half in the US), the
  ISRC and UPC registrant role, the ℗ notice, the funder's acknowledgement
  obligations, and every contract with a performer, producer or plant —
  [rights-fundamentals](../../rights/knowledge/rights-fundamentals.md).
- **What you give up.** The advance and the label's cash: production,
  manufacturing and marketing are paid from your own money or a grant.
- **What you keep.** Everything after the distributor's cut, and the masters.
  A licensed master that a label controls makes the label the funding
  applicant, "expected to retain exclusive control … for at least two years
  after first commercial release" (FACTOR, same page).
- **Not a legal entity by itself.** Incorporation, GST/QST and cross-border
  withholding are outside every department here; a W-8BEN is the one form
  [rights-fundamentals](../../rights/knowledge/rights-fundamentals.md) names.

## Where money enters and leaves

| Enters | Through | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Streams and downloads | the distributor, monthly, minus its cut | 69.6% of global recorded revenue (IFPI, above) |
| Physical | the plant's run sold at shows, direct and to stores | the margin is the artist's; the plant is paid up front |
| Neighbouring rights, maker side | Panorama / Re:Sound direct; SoundExchange | 50% of every tariff dollar in Canada and in the US ([rights-fundamentals](../../rights/knowledge/rights-fundamentals.md)) |
| Sync, master side | one deal at a time; no society | the song side is the writer's, separately |
| Grants | FACTOR, Musicaction, CALQ, Canada Council, SODEC | Canada only; each carries an acknowledgement line ([release-fundamentals § Credits](./release-fundamentals.md#credits)) |
| Live | the venue deal | agency money, not label money |

| Leaves | To | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Production | studio, engineers, artwork | recouped first under any label deal |
| Manufacturing | the plant; a mechanical licence for a cover on physical — 8.3 ¢ per copy under five minutes | [CMRRA](https://www.cmrra.ca/pay-as-you-press/), 2026-09-08 |
| Marketing, publicity, radio promotion | ads, a publicist, a promoter | [paid-promotion](../../digital-marketing/knowledge/paid-promotion.md) |
| Distribution | the distributor's fee or percentage | [distribution-fundamentals](../../distribution/knowledge/distribution-fundamentals.md) |
| Performers | session fees, then the society pays the rest | the fee scales are [`rights`](../../rights/knowledge/rights-fundamentals.md)'s |
| A missed funder line | 15% of the grant, or all of it | FACTOR and Musicaction, [release-fundamentals § Credits](./release-fundamentals.md#credits) |

**The deal shapes**, for an artist deciding whether to stay their own label.
No association publishes a standard contract; the ranges are practitioner
analysis ([Soundcharts](https://soundcharts.com/en/blog/splits-and-profits-record-deals-analysis), 2019, read 2026-09-09).

| Deal | Who owns the master | Artist's share | What is recouped first |
|---|---|---|---|
| Royalty deal | the label | 15–25% of net; Soundcharts models 15/85 | recording costs, from the artist's share |
| Profit share | the label, or shared | 50/50 after break-even | recording, promotion and marketing, from all income |
| Licence | the artist, for a term | higher, then the rights revert | the label's costs |
| Self-release | the artist | 100% minus the distributor | nothing — the artist paid |
