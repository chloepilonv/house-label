# Release fundamentals

The sequence — what happens when, and who does it — is
[release-path.md](./release-path.md). This file holds the reasons behind it.

## Singles before the record

- **One pitch per release, none once it is live.** Spotify: "You can only
  pitch one song at a time. Once your pitched song goes live, you can pitch
  another." and "Once a song has gone live, it's no longer eligible for
  pitching" ([Spotify for Artists](https://support.spotify.com/us/artists/article/pitching-music-and-videos-to-playlist-editors/), 2026-09-09).
  An album delivered whole gets one editorial shot; three singles get three.
- **The waterfall.** "A waterfall release strategy allows you to release an
  album or EP in increments, releasing individual singles that you can stack
  to direct traffic back to previous releases" — "at least a month of space
  between releases"; the streams carry over only if "the metadata and ISRC
  codes match the original track"
  ([CD Baby](https://diymusician.cdbaby.com/releasing-music/waterfall-release-strategy/), 2026-09-09).
  Three singles is the convention CD Baby describes; no platform publishes a
  right number.
- **The lead single is a scoping decision.** It is chosen before the upload,
  because the pitch needs the song in *Upcoming* and cannot be moved to a
  different track once that one is live.
- **Pre-saves convert.** "Nearly 70% of users who pre-save an album stream it
  in the first week" ([Spotify release guide](https://artists.spotify.com/en/blog/release-guide-preparing-for-release-day), 2026-09-09).

## Seasonality, and when to ignore it

| Window | What the data says | Source |
|---|---|---|
| June 1 – August 31 | Reggae over-indexes most, then Country and Afrobeats; R&B, Rap, Jazz and Classical "under-index in the summer" (US on-demand audio, weekly summer volume vs the 2022–2023 weekly average) | [Luminate, 2024-08-20](https://luminatedata.com/blog/summer-genres-summer-songs-in-the-u-s/) |
| November – early January | Holiday catalog takes the playlists and charts; every distributor's guide says wait for January unless the record is seasonal | practitioner convention — no platform publishes the effect size |
| Month one and after | "75% of a release's first-year streams happen after the first month" | [Spotify release guide](https://artists.spotify.com/en/blog/release-guide-preparing-for-release-day), 2026-09-09 |

- **Ignore the season when a harder date exists:** a funder's deadline, a
  booked show or showcase, a pressing-plant slot, a sync placement's air date.
  Each of those is absolute; the season is a bias.
- **The data is US-only.** Luminate measures US streams. No Canadian or
  Quebec body publishes a seasonality study (searched 2026-09-09); the Quebec
  calendar's fixed points are the funders' deadlines and the festival
  application windows in
  [presskit-fundamentals](../../presskit/knowledge/presskit-fundamentals.md).

## Coordination: one plan, one calendar, one Drive

- **One plan.** Every step has one owner and one date. Press works 8 weeks
  before to 8 after ([press-fundamentals](../../press/knowledge/press-fundamentals.md)),
  marketing 4 before to 4 after ([marketing-fundamentals](../../digital-marketing/knowledge/marketing-fundamentals.md)),
  radio 6–8 weeks before ([radio-pitch](../../promotion/knowledge/radio-pitch.md)).
  Each window is right on its own; the plan is what stops them from colliding
  on the same week with the same asset.
- **One calendar.** Every date is an offset from the street date, so moving
  the date moves the plan. The only absolute dates are the ones nobody here
  controls: a funder's deadline, a booked show, a plant's slot.
- **One Drive.** One release folder — audio, artwork, lyrics, credits,
  invoices — that the presskit, the upload and the press send all read from.
  A journalist gets one private, non-expiring streaming link, never a
  download folder ([Exclaim!](https://exclaim.ca/contact/getcoverage), 2026-08-14).
- **Milestones here, tasks there.** "The presskit is finished" is the plan's;
  "post the teaser Tuesday" is marketing's.

## Credits

### ℗ and ©

| Line | Covers | Owner | Form |
|---|---|---|---|
| **℗** | the sound recording (the master) | whoever paid for and owns the recording — the artist, or their label | "the symbol ℗ … the year of first publication of the sound recording … the name of the owner of copyright in the sound recording" ([17 U.S.C. §402(b)](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid%3AUSC-prelim-title17-section402&num=0&edition=prelim), 2026-09-09) |
| **©** | the composition — and the artwork, liner text | the writers or their publisher; the designer or photographer for the art | `© YYYY holder` |

- Two lines, two owners. A self-releasing writer-performer holds both; a
  cover song's © is someone else's; a licensed master's ℗ is the licensor's.
- Canada attaches no notice requirement — copyright exists on creation and
  registration is voluntary evidence
  ([CIPO](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/canadian-intellectual-property-office/en/what-you-should-know-about-copyright), 2026-08-27).
  The notice still goes on, because the stores and the plant ask for it.
- The distributor's ℗ field is in
  [delivery-requirements](../../distribution/knowledge/delivery-requirements.md).

### Contributor roles

Credits travel in the distributor's DDEX delivery, in DDEX's role vocabulary,
which is why the release folder stores them in those words.

| DDEX class | Who | Example roles | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **DisplayArtist** | the names the store shows | `MainArtist`, `FeaturedArtist` | [DDEX KB](https://kb.ddex.net/implementing-each-standard/best-practices-for-all-ddex-standards/guidance-on-contributors,-artists-and-writers/information-on-displayartists,-displayartistnames,-contributors-and-indirectcontributors/), 2026-09-09 |
| **Contributor** | "individuals that were in the studio when the recording or mixing took place" | `Producer`, `StudioProducer`, `RecordingEngineer`, `MixingEngineer`, `MasteringEngineer`, `Musician` + instrument, `GroupMember` | same page; instruments in [roles and instrumentation](https://kb.ddex.net/implementing-each-standard/best-practices-for-all-ddex-standards/guidance-on-contributors,-artists-and-writers/roles-and-instrumentation-in-performances/) |
| **IndirectContributor** | writers of the song — "Composers and arrangers are … no direct contributor roles" | `Composer`, `Lyricist`, `ComposerLyricist`, `Arranger` | same page |

- **Spotify** shows "all credits from the metadata your label or distributor
  sends us"; a fix is a resend by the distributor; "Some distributors don't
  support credits yet" ([Spotify](https://support.spotify.com/us/artists/article/song-credits/), 2026-09-09).
- **Apple Music** groups credits as "Performers, Composition and Lyrics, and
  Production and Engineering", delivered by the label or distributor through
  iTunes Connect or Transporter, live in about five business days
  ([Apple Music Provider Support](https://itunespartner.apple.com/music/support/5542-deliver-credits), 2026-09-09).
  New releases need at least one Performer and one Production & Engineering
  credit per track — Apple's specification sits behind a partner login; the
  rule is restated by distributors
  ([Amuse](https://support.amuse.io/en/articles/277215-apple-music-credits), 2026-09-09).

### Who is paid, through which society

A credit line is also a claim. The join links, costs and split rules are
[rights-fundamentals](../../rights/knowledge/rights-fundamentals.md); this
table says only which credit opens which door.

| Credit | Right it triggers | Quebec · Canada | United States |
|---|---|---|---|
| Composer, lyricist, arranger | performance + mechanical | SOCAN (performance); SOCAN RR or CMRRA (mechanical) | ASCAP or BMI; The MLC |
| Featured performer | neighbouring, performer side | Artisti, ACTRA RACS, or Re:Sound direct — the choice is not geographic | SoundExchange (45% of the statutory pool) |
| Session and backing performer, with instrument | neighbouring, non-featured pool — 20% of the performer half in Canada, 5% fund in the US | same societies; needs an IPN | AFM & SAG-AFTRA Fund via SoundExchange |
| ℗ holder (master owner) | neighbouring, maker side — the other 50% | Panorama or Re:Sound direct | SoundExchange |
| Producer, engineers | none — paid by contract, or by points off the master | — | — |
| Artwork, photography | © of the image; a credit line | — | — |

- A performer credited with no IPN, or not credited at all, is money that
  cannot be matched to them; the society matches on IPN and ISRC.

## Gates

What blocks what. A missing item with no gate behind it is a note, not a
blocker.

| This | is blocked until | Why |
|---|---|---|
| The mix | splits are agreed in writing | after there is a record to argue over it is a negotiation ([rights-fundamentals](../../rights/knowledge/rights-fundamentals.md)) |
| The pressing order | a mechanical licence for any cover on a physical format | CMRRA's Pay-As-You-Press application needs the pressing confirmation, minimum 500 units per format ([CMRRA](https://www.cmrra.ca/pay-as-you-press/), 2026-09-08) |
| The full pressing run | the test pressing is approved | the plant's own gate ([Microforum](https://www.microforum.ca/blog/how-to-get-an-album-pressed-on-vinyl)) |
| The upload | masters, artwork, ISRC/UPC, writer and performer credits, ℗ line, every sample cleared | the distributor asks for all of it at once ([CD Baby](https://diymusician.cdbaby.com/releasing-music/music-distribution-guide/)); a credit added later is a resend |
| The editorial pitch | the release is in *Upcoming* on Spotify for Artists — so the upload, and the profile claimed | the form lives there ([Spotify](https://support.spotify.com/us/artists/article/pitching-music-and-videos-to-playlist-editors/)) |
| Countdown Page and pre-save | delivery, then up to 72 hours | Spotify creates the option from the delivered release |
| Registrations paid on week one | works and recordings filed with the codes before release day | societies match plays on ISRC and IPN |
| The street date itself | a funder's deadline, if the record is to be funded | the funder's calendar does not move |
| Independent curators, Pandora (US) | release day — a live link | they take nothing unreleased ([playlist-pitch](../../promotion/knowledge/playlist-pitch.md)) |
