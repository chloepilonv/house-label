# Outreach law

Obeyed by `press` and `promotion`. Not legal advice.

## I. Quebec

**I.i Privacy — RLRQ c. P-39.1, art. 1** ([Loi 25, art. 100](https://www.publicationsduquebec.gouv.qc.ca/fileadmin/Fichiers_client/lois_et_reglements/LoisAnnuelles/fr/2021/2021C25F.PDF))
> « Elles ne s'appliquent pas non plus aux renseignements personnels qui concernent l'exercice par la personne concernée d'une fonction au sein d'une entreprise, tels que son nom, son titre et sa fonction, de même que l'adresse, l'adresse de courrier électronique et le numéro de téléphone de son lieu de travail. »

→ Work email: yes. Personal email (Gmail…): **no** — recommend Instagram or their submission form.

**I.ii Privacy — art. 12**
> « ne peut être considérée comme une fin compatible la prospection commerciale ou philanthropique. »

→ A personal address collected for one purpose can't be reused to pitch. Fine up to $100,000 for an individual (art. 91).

**I.iii Anti-spam — CASL** applies: see II.ii.

## II. Canada

**II.i Privacy — PIPEDA, s. 4.01** ([S.C. 2000, c. 5](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/P-8.6/page-1.html))
> "This Part does not apply to an organization in respect of the business contact information of an individual that the organization collects, uses or discloses solely for the purpose of communicating or facilitating communication with the individual in relation to their employment, business or profession."

→ Work email: yes. Personal email: **no** — recommend Instagram.

**II.ii Anti-spam — CASL, s. 10(9)(b)** ([S.C. 2010, c. 23](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/E-1.6/FullText.html))
> "the person to whom the message is sent has conspicuously published … the electronic address …, the publication is not accompanied by a statement that the person does not wish to receive unsolicited commercial electronic messages … and the message is relevant to the person's business, role, functions or duties"

→ One person, about their beat. Never a list. Keep the page it was published on (`artist.contacts.source_url`).

**II.iii Anti-spam — CASL, s. 6(2) + [SOR/2012-36, s. 2(1)(d)](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2012-36/FullText.html)**
> "set out prescribed information that identifies the person who sent the message … [and] an unsubscribe mechanism"; "the mailing address, and either a telephone number …, an email address or a web address"

→ Every email ends with the footer below.

**II.iv Anti-spam — CASL, s. 11(3)**
> "no later than 10 business days after the indication has been sent"

→ "Stop" is recorded the same day (`opted_out_at`). Never written to again. Up to $1,000,000 for an individual (s. 20(4)).

**II.v DMs — CASL, s. 1(1)**
> "electronic address means an address used in connection with the transmission of an electronic message to (a) an electronic mail account; (b) an instant messaging account; … or (d) any similar account."

→ An Instagram DM obeys II.ii–II.iv too. Too short for the footer? SOR/2012-36 s. 2(2): the information "may be posted on a page on the World Wide Web … by means of a link" — the "stop" line plus a link to the artist's site.

## III. United States

**III.i Privacy — CCPA** ([CPPA FAQ](https://cppa.ca.gov/faq.html), read 2026-09-28)
> "Have a gross annual revenue of $26.625 million or more"

→ An independent artist is outside it. Same rule anyway: work email yes, personal no.

**III.ii Anti-spam — CAN-SPAM** ([FTC guide](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business), read 2026-09-28)
> "The law makes no exception for business-to-business email." "Your message must include your valid physical postal address." "Honor a recipient's opt-out request within 10 business days."

→ The same footer. Up to $53,088 per email.
